Frequently asked questions

What should I prepare for an astrology consultation?

Your birth date, approximate or exact birth time, birthplace and questions may be helpful.

Can I ask about Vastu for an existing home?

Yes. A floor plan and information about the property's orientation can help guide the discussion.

Does Reiki replace medical treatment?

No. Reiki is a complementary wellness practice and is not a substitute for professional medical care.
