FAQs

Questions? We got you.

Is this the same as a daily horoscope?

No. A consultation is a personal conversation about your own chart and questions, not a generic reading for your sun sign.

I'm totally new to this. Is that okay?

Absolutely. You don't need any background, just bring your curiosity and your questions.

What should I prepare for an astrology consultation?

Your birth date, approximate or exact birth time, birthplace and your questions.

Can I ask about Vastu for a rented flat or existing home?

Yes. A floor plan and information about the property's orientation can help guide the discussion.

Does Reiki replace medical treatment?

No. Reiki is a complementary wellness practice and is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health care.
